Checking this layer…

This lets the current Peakbagger tab load this layer using your saved parameters. BPB sends your key to the provider and returns map data to Peakbagger.

Hiding the key does not stop page scripts from requesting tiles and using your provider quota. This approval limits the tab to 20,000 requests for 8 hours.

Closing or reloading the map ends approval. BPB also limits each provider to 25,000 requests per hour and 100,000 per day across tabs.

Your browser may also ask for provider access. That permission lets BPB contact the provider; this approval controls which tab may use your key.
