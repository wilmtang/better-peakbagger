      Upload to ImgBB

      ImgBB hosts the image at a public link. This browser sends it directly, using your own free API key.

      How to get your key — about 15 seconds with Google or another third-party sign-in.

      The saved key remains in device-local extension storage. It is given only to Better Peakbagger's exact packaged photo page for the direct ImgBB upload and is never exposed to Peakbagger, another website, GitHub, browser sync, or status UI. Manual exports can include it; keep them private.

      No key? Export the image and paste a link from any host into your report.
